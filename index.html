Questions

What people ask before they install it.

See privacy notes

How do I change the key of a song on Spotify?

Pick Spotify as the source and press − or +. Each step is one semitone.

Can I change the pitch without changing the tempo?

Yes. Up to 12 semitones either way, plus 50 cents of fine tuning, at the same speed.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app that plays sound, a video call too, or all sound on your Mac at once.

Does PitchTap put video out of sync?

Slightly: the shift takes about 0.15 seconds, so lips can move a moment early.

Is PitchTap free?

Yes. It is open source under GPL-3.0, with no ads, account or in-app purchases.

Does PitchTap record or upload my audio?

No. The sound is shifted on your Mac and played straight back. It goes online only to check for updates.
