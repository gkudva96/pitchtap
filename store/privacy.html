PitchTap Privacy Policy

Applies to the PitchTap Chrome extension and the PitchTap Mac app. Last updated 19 October 2026.

In short: PitchTap does not collect, store or share any of your data.

Audio

To change the pitch of what you're listening to, PitchTap processes the audio of the browser tab you choose (in Chrome) or the app you choose (on a Mac). The audio is processed on your own computer, in memory, as it plays. It is never recorded, saved to disk or sent anywhere.

Settings

PitchTap remembers your on/off switch, pitch and fine-tune settings so they're the same next time. These are stored only on your computer, in Chrome's extension storage or the Mac's app preferences.

What PitchTap does not do

  • It makes no network connections and has no server.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • It does not read the pages you visit or your browsing history.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone, because it has none.

Permissions

The Chrome extension asks for four permissions: tab capture and active tab, to receive the audio of the tab you click PitchTap on; offscreen, to keep processing that audio after the popup closes; and storage, for your settings.
